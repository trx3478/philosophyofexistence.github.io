PHILOSOPHY OF EXISTENCE

“To exist is to be between two voids...”

1. THERE IS A CREATOR OF THE UNIVERSE.
“This tremendous level of order cannot be explained by coincidence.”
2. THE CREATOR IS ONE.
“If there were multiple creators, they would not consciously establish a system like 'evil' that damages their own order.”
3. THE CREATOR CREATES BUT DOES NOT DESTROY.
“Because, as the name implies, they are the 'creator'; destroying is not their attribute.”
4. TIME DOES NOT ACTUALLY EXIST.
“The beginning and the end occurred simultaneously; we are merely experiencing the sub-fractions of time.”
5. HUMAN CONSCIOUSNESS IS LIMITED.
“The Creator did not give the knowledge completely; although questioning is left free, access is restricted.”
6. EVOLUTION ALONE CANNOT EXPLAIN EVERYTHING.
“There is no feedback mechanism that retroactively corrects the output generated during the process.”
7. EXISTENCE HAS A PURPOSE.
“Every event in the universe produces energy, creates action-reaction, and this cycle points to an ultimate outcome.”
8. IF THERE IS AN OUTCOME, THERE MUST BE AN EVALUATION.
“If the Creator is watching, they must react at some point; this gives rise to the idea of a cosmic 'reckoning'.”
9. IF THE CREATOR KNOWS THE OUTCOME, WHY IS THERE A PROCESS?
“The process is a mandatory stage for consciousness; however, the reason for this necessity remains unsolved.”
10. GOOD AND EVIL HAVE BEEN CONSCIOUSLY INCLUDED IN THE SYSTEM.
11. HUMANS CANNOT BE HELD FULLY RESPONSIBLE FOR ANYTHING.
“It is the Creator who creates the conditions, limits, and possibilities.”
12. JUST AS THE RIGHT TO HOLD ACCOUNTABLE BELONGS TO THE CREATOR, HUMANS ALSO HAVE THE RIGHT TO HOLD THE CREATOR ACCOUNTABLE.
13. THE "OBLIGATION TO CREATE AND NOT DESTROY" IS DIRECTED TOWARDS A GREATER WHOLE.
“There may be other wholes within this greater whole; but there is no conflict among them. This strengthens the idea of a single creator.”
14. THE FINAL QUESTION REMAINS VALID: WHY DOES THE CREATOR CREATE?
“No model can fully explain this question.”
